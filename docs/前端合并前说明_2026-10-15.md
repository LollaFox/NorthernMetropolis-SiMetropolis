## Original H3 rendering delivery (historical)

Neutral white building materials, light grey ground/background, subdued water and vegetation, neutral lighting and existing PCF soft shadows. Facade textures and decorative traffic are suppressed in white mode. Cyan baseline and gold additional policy flows retain their colours and original scale. Geometry, policy rules and passenger counts are unchanged.

Open **? → Map appearance** to switch between **White study model** and **Original materials**. Reload defaults to white. Original 8892 has not been overwritten. Browser case storage is isolated by port, so 8892 cases do not appear in 8893 automatically.

This is a Three.js presentation template inspired by Rhino Arctic, not a Rhino `.3dm` file, a GIS-accurate city, or a full ambient occlusion renderer. Reference: https://docs.mcneel.com/rhino/8mac/help/en-us/options/view_displaymodes_arctic.htm

## Files and integration

- `sandbox/web/white-model.js`: reversible material and lighting adapter. Restores instance colours and visibility; excludes policy overlays and selection; caches and disposes owned materials.
- `sandbox/web/white-model.css`: contrast adjustments only.
- `sandbox/web/city.js`: constructor/update/disposal hooks.
- `sandbox/web/app.js` and `index.html`: appearance selector in existing help menu.
- `sandbox/tests/test_white_model.mjs`: overlay preservation and update/restoration regressions.

For future material changes, call prepareUpdate before changing materials and refresh afterwards. Add new analytical mesh overlays to the exclusion set. Keep global scene overrideMaterial unset. Shared material classification may need refinement if context objects acquire additional semantic layers.
