SOURCE-BASED SUMMARY · RULE MAPPING

New Huanggang Port opening

Policy meaning Passenger clearance moves to the redeveloped port with co-location arrangements and 24-hour services. The published clearance-time target is approximately 5 minutes, compared with around 30 minutes.

Groups considered Commuters, family/visitor trips and business passengers. This study does not simulate freight or eligibility to cross the boundary.

Modeled mechanism Clearance savings and information reach change assumed trip demand. Feeder capacity limits the additional trips that can be served.

Outside this model Housing, migration, employment creation, other-port diversion and seasonal demand. The interpretation is curated; it is not a generated legal or policy assessment.

Official public opening: 12 Oct 2026 at 06:30. A hypothetical opening uses the first month you apply the scenario. Government targets are not measured outcomes.
